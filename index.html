    End-to-End Revenue Cycle Management

    You do not need separate vendors for front-desk work and medical billing. One team manages the full patient journey: booking appointments, verifying coverage, submitting clean claims, posting payments and following up on overdue balances.

    Fewer Missed Calls & Appointments

    A missed call is often a lost patient. Your healthcare VA answers patient calls, confirms appointments, handles cancellations and reschedules, and keeps your EHR calendar up to date. That keeps your schedule full and no-shows low.

    A/R Follow-Up & Older Balance Recovery

    Practices often have significant revenue sitting in unpaid claims that are 60, 90 or 120+ days old. Our A/R specialists review every underpaid or denied claim, contact payers directly, file appeals within timely filing limits and follow up courteously with patients on outstanding balances.

    HIPAA Security & Clear Accountability

    Protecting patient health information (PHI) is central to how we work. Our teams work from facilities with biometric entry, use encrypted VPN connections and role-based access, and follow a paperless policy. Every engagement is covered by a signed BAA.
