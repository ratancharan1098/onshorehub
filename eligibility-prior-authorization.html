Many denials can be prevented before the visit. We verify every patient's coverage and benefits in advance and secure prior authorizations 48 hours before the appointment, so your team is never surprised at check-in or at billing.

We check active coverage, deductibles, co-pays and co-insurance, track referrals, and submit and follow up on prior authorizations with each payer. Everything is documented in your EHR, so your front desk can collect the right amount and your billers send claims that match the authorization.

What's included

Eligibility verification

Active coverage confirmed for every scheduled patient before the visit.

Benefits breakdown

Deductible, co-pay and co-insurance details ready for your front desk.

Referral tracking

Referrals requested, tracked and attached before specialist visits.

Prior authorizations

Submitted and approved 48 hours before the scheduled service.

Authorization follow-up

Pending requests chased with the payer until a decision is made.

EHR documentation

Verification and authorization details recorded where your team can see them.
