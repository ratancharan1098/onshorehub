Your dedicated billing team takes over the full claim cycle, from charge entry to the final payment, so your front desk and providers can focus on patients instead of payers.

We work inside your existing EHR and practice management system, submit claims electronically through your clearinghouse every day, and follow each one until it is paid. When a claim is denied, we fix the cause and resubmit it, so the same mistake does not keep costing you money.

What's included

Charge entry

Accurate charge capture from encounter notes and superbills, checked before submission.

Claim submission

Daily electronic submission through your clearinghouse, with claim scrubbing to catch errors first.

Denial management

Every denial reviewed, corrected and resubmitted or appealed, with the root cause logged.

Payment posting

ERA and EOB posting, reconciliation against deposits, and flagged underpayments.

Secondary billing

Secondary and tertiary claims filed promptly after the primary payer pays.

Monthly KPI reports

Collections, denial rate, days in A/R and clean claim rate, explained in plain language.

Who it's for

Built for independent physicians, multi-specialty clinics, and dental and mental health practices across the USA that want a reliable remote back-office without hiring in-house.

Every engagement runs under HIPAA-compliant workflows with a signed Business Associate Agreement, and your dedicated team works during your clinic hours.
